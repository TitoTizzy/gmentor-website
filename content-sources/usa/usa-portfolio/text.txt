[Normal] Greetings, I am Marie Gaëlle Mentor, an architect with a diverse portfolio that spans both local and international realms, primarily focused on residential constructions. I embarked on my educational journey with a Bachelor’s in Architecture from GOC University in Port-au-Prince, Haiti, laying the groundwork for a career marked by innovation and unwavering dedication.
[Normal] During the initial years, I played pivotal roles as an Architectural Designer and Site Supervisor with Gecim Construction in Haiti from February 1995 to August 1997, specializing in crafting residential spaces within my homeland. Venturing beyond Haiti, I embraced challenges and opportunities on the global architectural stage, showcasing my adaptability and proficiency in residential design during my tenure as an Architectural Designer in the United States, particularly with AWA.
[Normal] Transitioning into a freelance role, I now operate under the banner of Marie Gaelle Mentor Architectural Designer LLC, embodying an entrepreneurial spirit rooted in creating distinctive residential spaces. My journey extends beyond traditional borders, as I have had the privilege of collaborating with international entities such as Doctors Without Borders and Petits frères et Sœurs, further enriching my global perspective.
[Normal] One of my notable international projects is the 2018 PNUD project in Port de Paix, where I designed an office space for the community, emphasizing functionality, sustainability, and aesthetics. These experiences underscore my versatility and resilience, earning recognition and respect on the global stage, particularly in the realm of residential constructions.
[Normal] My portfolio reflects a commitment to pushing boundaries, creating architectural marvels that seamlessly blend local expertise with a global perspective. Special emphasis is placed on crafting spaces where people genuinely feel at home, encapsulating a unique expression of creativity, cultural fusion, and the art of comfortable living. Join me on a journey where each residential project is a testament to international collaboration and innovative design.

[Normal] As the architect behind this modern multi-story residential structure, my team and I have strived to embody professionalism and a deep commitment to fulfilling the client's vision. Our design showcases a sleek, minimalist aesthetic with clean lines and a vertical orientation, maximizing space efficiency while adhering to contemporary architectural principles. We've cladded the building in durable, low-maintenance horizontal blue-grey vinyl siding, ensuring both longevity and visual appeal. Symmetrically placed windows on each floor optimize natural light and ventilation, promoting energy efficiency and enhancing the occupants' comfort. The addition of a gable roof not only adds to the structure's aesthetic appeal but also offers practical benefits such as efficient water drainage and increased attic space. Despite the ongoing construction, characterized by visible dirt and unfinished landscaping, our dedication to excellence shines through as we continue to evolve and refine the project to meet the client's needs and aspirations.

[Normal] Throughout the construction process of the Central Extension Unit (UCE) office extension, my team and I have approached every challenge with professionalism and dedication. Despite the project's lack of high technical complexity, we recognized the importance of careful planning and execution due to the unique constraints of the site. Our primary focus was to seamlessly integrate the new structure with the existing irregularly shaped pool while ensuring the preservation of surrounding trees and shrubs. Constructing directly over the existing pool required precision and innovation to achieve both functional and aesthetic harmony. Despite the difficulties posed by the site's access and potential future relocation needs, we remained committed to fulfilling the client's vision. Our goal was not only to meet but to exceed expectations, delivering a lightweight structure that seamlessly extends the UCE office while respecting the natural environment. This project stands as a testament to our professionalism, creativity, and unwavering dedication to client satisfaction.
